# Youngwoo Lee

I'm an undergraduate at Seoul National University, majoring in History Education and Economics. I previously worked as an Equity Derivatives Sales Trainee at Société Générale Securities Korea.

My interests lie at the intersection of financial markets and quantitative modeling — particularly in interest rates, foreign exchange, derivatives, and asset pricing.

**Almost Surely** is my personal research notebook, where I explore questions about financial markets and the mathematics behind them through theoretical notes, market analysis, and numerical experiments.
